Hodag Trail

Run as the legendary Hodag through the pine trails of Rhinelander, Wisconsin. Jump the logs, duck the leaning pines, take the turns, and see how far you can get before Paul Bunyan and Babe catch up.

Hodag Trail is an endless runner for Android, coming to Google Play. Your best runs go on the world high score board through Google Play Games.
