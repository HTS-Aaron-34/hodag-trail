Hodag Trail Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Hodag Trail collects very little about you: your game progress stays on your phone, only your scores and the name you pick for the world high score board go online, and if you choose to watch a video ad, Google's ad service receives information about your device to show it.

Who we are

Hodag Trail is a mobile game made by First Prize Ventures LLC, in Illinois, USA ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Hodag Trail app uses, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Hodag Trail app for Android distributed through Google Play.

Information kept only on your device

The game saves the following on your phone so it can remember your progress. It is never sent to us.

  • High score board (this phone): your top 5 scores and the names you type in for them
  • Coins and extra-life use
  • Trail stats: totals such as runs, distance, time played and wipeouts
  • Settings: music and sound on or off

We have no access to this information. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes it.

The world high score board

Hodag Trail's world high score board runs on Unity Gaming Services, provided by Unity Technologies. When the game starts, it signs in to the board with an anonymous player ID that Unity creates for this copy of the game. There is no account to make and no sign-in button. The game sends Unity:

  • the anonymous player ID
  • the name you pick for the board (you choose it; it does not need to be your real name, and we ask that it isn't)
  • your scores, so they can be ranked

Other players can see your board name, score and rank. Unity processes this information for us to run the board, and Unity's privacy policy also applies. We can see the board, and we remove names that are rude or that look like real personal information. We never ask for your email address, phone number or real name, and the board isn't linked to your Google account.

Purchases

Coins can be bought inside the game. All purchases are handled by Google Play. Your payment details go to Google, not to us; we only receive confirmation that a purchase was made so the game can add your coins. Google Play's terms and Google's Privacy Policy apply to purchases.

Optional video ads

Hodag Trail never shows ads on its own. You can choose to watch a short video ad to earn a coin, after a run or in the coin shop. These ads come from Google AdMob. When the game loads or plays an ad, Google's ad software collects:

  • your IP address, which can show your approximate location
  • your device's advertising ID and similar device and app identifiers
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps
  • diagnostic information about the app and the ad's performance

Google uses this to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. It is sent encrypted. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time with Ad privacy choices on the How to Play page. Whether ads are personalized depends on your choices and settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off personalized ads, in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services apply to ads.

Crash reports, tracking and selling data

  • Crash and diagnostic data: if the game crashes, Google Play and the Unity game engine may collect technical details, such as device model, Android version and the error, to help fix problems. This does not identify you by name.
  • No tracking by us: Hodag Trail itself has no analytics and we do not track you across other apps or websites. Google's ad service may use your advertising ID as described above.
  • No selling: we never sell your information. The only information shared for advertising is what Google's ad service collects, as described above.

Children

Hodag Trail is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has put their real name or other personal information on the world high score board, contact us and we will remove it.

Keeping and deleting your information

  • On your phone: uninstall the app or clear its storage to delete everything it saved.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it at any time in your phone's settings.
  • World high score board: your best score and board name are kept until you ask us to remove them. Email us with your board name and we will delete your score, name and player ID from the board. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage gives you a new anonymous player ID, but doesn't remove your old score from the board.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts using information in a new way, we will update this policy and change the date at the top before the update reaches players.
