Hodag Trail Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

Hodag Trail collects very little about you: your game progress stays on your phone, and only your score and Google Play Games name go online, for the world high score board.

Who we are

Hodag Trail is a mobile game made by First Prize Ventures LLC, in Illinois, USA ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Hodag Trail app uses, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Hodag Trail app for Android distributed through Google Play.

Information kept only on your device

The game saves the following on your phone so it can remember your progress. It is never sent to us.

  • High score board (this phone): your top 5 scores and the names you type in for them
  • Coins and extra-life use
  • Trail stats: totals such as runs, distance, time played and wipeouts
  • Settings: music and sound on or off

We have no access to this information. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes it.

Google Play Games and the world high score board

Hodag Trail uses Google Play Games Services for its world high score board. When you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game sends Google:

  • your Play Games player ID and gamer name (your Play Games profile name, not your real name or email address)
  • your scores, so Google can rank them on the board

Other players can see your gamer name, score and rank on the board. Google operates this service, and Google's Privacy Policy and Play Games settings apply to it. We see the same public board other players see. We do not receive your email address, contacts or other Google account information.

You can play without signing in to Play Games; your scores then stay on your phone only.

Purchases

Coins can be bought inside the game. All purchases are handled by Google Play. Your payment details go to Google, not to us; we only receive confirmation that a purchase was made so the game can add your coins. Google Play's terms and Google's Privacy Policy apply to purchases.

Crash reports, ads and selling data

  • Crash and diagnostic data: if the game crashes, Google Play and the Unity game engine may collect technical details, such as device model, Android version and the error, to help fix problems. This does not identify you by name.
  • No ads: Hodag Trail shows no advertising and contains no ad trackers.
  • No analytics profiles: we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing: we never sell your information or share it for advertising.

Children

Hodag Trail is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used Play Games with this game in a way that shared their information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Keeping and deleting your information

  • On your phone: uninstall the app or clear its storage to delete everything it saved.
  • World high score board: Google keeps your best score while your Play Games profile exists. You can delete your Play Games data for this game, or your whole Play Games profile, in the Google Play Games settings. You can also email us and we will ask Google to remove your score.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts using information in a new way, we will update this policy and change the date at the top before the update reaches players.
